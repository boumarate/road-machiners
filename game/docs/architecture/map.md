# Map and world

- The map is baked offline, never built in the game. `src/mapgen/bake.ts` runs fixed layers over one draft: base relief, geology, road and site finish, the old world, the new world, territories, ground types and boulders.
- `src/mapgen/newworld.ts` grows scrub from moist ground, fills small basins with dirty water or toxic pools, and places shack camps with fences and junk, and car wrecks, with numbers in `NEW_WORLD`.
- `src/mapgen/oldworld.ts` places ruined settlements, overlook and bend buildings, old asphalt roads, old highways with broken bridges over deep gorges, power lines, billboards, tank hulks and dead fields, with numbers in `OLD_WORLD` in `src/data/terrain.ts`.
- `src/mapgen/geology.ts` holds the rain, slump, wind and dune rules, with numbers in `GEOLOGY` in `src/data/terrain.ts`.
- A territory is a location of kind `territory` in `REGION.locations`: no edge, gates, pads or `site` obstacle, no flattening and no hardpan, so trucks drive in over the natural ground. Its rules live in `TERRITORIES` in `src/data/territory.ts`: the authored hull pieces, caches, tracks and reactor with its hazard, and the drawn patches, rim rocks and scree. The Fallen Sun's numbers are measured in the level concept's pixels, and each comment names them.
- `src/sim/territory.ts` answers every territory question for the sim: membership, spots, entries, hunting grounds and hazard zones. It also turns the authored layout into map tiles (`territoryPieces`, `territoryCaches`, `territoryTracks`, `reactorPos`), so the bake, the render and the tests place things alike. The reactor's position is the hazard's centre. `src/sim/hazard.ts` drains driver health inside a zone, and the nav layer blocks zones for routes only.
- `src/mapgen/territory.ts` fills each territory. It seats the pieces first, then places them, the reactor and the caches, then draws the rim rocks and each patch's field spots and debris. Spots come before debris, so debris never boxes one in. Drawn props keep clear of the pieces' boxes, the tracks, the roads and the hazard.
  - The seat levels the ground under each piece's low boxes to the height at its centre and eases it back to the relief over a few tiles, so a long piece neither floats nor sinks.
  - The ribcage tube and the arch shells are hollow models. Their roofs start above `PHYSICS.truckClearance`, so physics, nav, sight and free-spot checks see only their walls, and trucks drive through with no special case.
  - `src/three/render/crater-tracks.ts` drapes twin ruts on the baked heights along each track. It is decoration only, not a road.
- Baked props stay out of saves and are rebuilt from the map file on load. `decodeMap()` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` reads the file. Boot fetches it in `src/three/main.ts`, and tests use `TEST_MAP` from `src/test/map.ts`.
- `src/sim/bridge.ts` holds the Canyon Bridge geometry. The map stays one level. `heightAt` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` returns the deck height on the deck, and `groundAt` returns the canyon floor under it. Both rails block routes and physics, so trucks get on only over the ends.
- `src/sim/weather.ts` answers `weatherAt`, which every weather effect reads. `src/sim/sun.ts` owns time of day and sun heat, and `src/sim/engine-heat.ts` the player's engine heat. `src/sim/vision.ts` owns sight and fog of war, and `src/sim/locations.ts` discovery.
